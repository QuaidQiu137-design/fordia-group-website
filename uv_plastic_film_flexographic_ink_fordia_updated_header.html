UV Plastic Film Flexographic Ink

Specialized UV ink system engineered for non-porous plastic films including PE, PP, and PET. Delivers exceptional adhesion, scratch resistance, and flexibility for demanding shrink sleeve and flexible packaging applications.

Engineered Performance

Our UV Flexo Ink for plastic films is formulated with advanced photoinitiators and specialized resins to overcome the adhesion challenges inherent in non-porous substrates, ensuring absolute graphic integrity under stress.
