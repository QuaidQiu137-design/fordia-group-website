FORDIA Calibrated Underpacking Paper

Engineered for precise impression cylinder pressure, ensuring absolute consistency and superior offset printing quality across high-volume runs.

Precision Underpacking for Flawless Impressions

FORDIA Calibrated Underpacking Paper is the critical foundation for high-end offset printing. By providing a strictly controlled, uniform thickness beneath the printing blanket or plate, it ensures exact pressure transfer across the entire impression cylinder.

Manufactured to tolerances of ±0.01mm, it resists compression over long print runs, prevents dot gain, and maintains crisp image resolution. Engineered for both sheet-fed and web offset environments, FORDIA guarantees stability where it matters most.
