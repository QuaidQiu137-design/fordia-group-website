Flexo Water Based Ink for Paper, Label and Film

High-performance, environmentally responsible aqueous ink systems engineered for precision flexographic printing across a diverse range of porous and non-porous substrates.

Eco-Friendly Formulation High Adhesion

Versatile Aqueous Technology

Our advanced flexographic water-based inks are formulated for optimal performance on narrow and wide web presses. Utilizing state-of-the-art polymer emulsions, these inks deliver exceptional color density and stable rheology during high-speed runs.

Engineered for versatility, the system provides superior wetting and adhesion not only on highly porous corrugated and tissue materials but also on challenging non-porous films and synthetic labels, ensuring sharp dot reproduction and minimal dot gain.
