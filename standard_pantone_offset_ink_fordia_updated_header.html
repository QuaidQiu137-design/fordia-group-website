Standard Pantone Offset Ink

High-performance, precision-formulated offset printing inks designed for exact color matching and exceptional runnability on modern high-speed presses. Engineered for corporate branding consistency.

Product Overview

palette

Exact Color Fidelity

Formulated to strictly adhere to the Pantone Matching System (PMS) standards, ensuring 100% brand color consistency across all print runs globally.

speed

High-Speed Performance

Optimized tack and viscosity profiles allow for exceptional runnability on modern sheet-fed offset presses exceeding 18,000 sheets per hour.

eco

Sustainable Base

Manufactured with vegetable oil-based vehicles, minimizing VOC emissions while maintaining rapid setting times and excellent rub resistance.
