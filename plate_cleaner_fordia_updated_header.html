FORDIA Plate Cleaner

High-efficiency industrial cleaner formulated for the rapid removal of ink, oxidation, and stubborn residues from CTP plates. Engineered for precision material safety and peak performance.

Technical Advantages

speed

Rapid Action Formula

Penetrates heavy ink buildup and oxidation instantly, significantly reducing press downtime during maintenance cycles.

shield

Material Safe

Non-abrasive formulation designed specifically to protect sensitive CTP plate coatings, extending plate longevity.

eco

Low VOC Emissions

Compliant with stringent industrial environmental standards, ensuring a safer workspace for technicians.

verified

Residue-Free

Leaves absolutely no oily film behind, guaranteeing optimal ink receptivity upon immediate press restart.

Primary Applications

  • check_circle

    Routine Maintenance

  • check_circle

  • check_circle

    Pre-Storage Preparation

    Applying a protective clean layer before short-term plate archiving.
