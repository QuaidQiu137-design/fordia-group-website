Water-based Flexo Ink for PP / PE Cup

Advanced aqueous formulation engineered specifically for non-absorbent polyolefin substrates. Delivers exceptional rub resistance, immediate adhesion, and brilliant color density without the environmental impact of solvent-based alternatives.

Viscosity 25-35 Zahn #2
Drying Speed High-Speed Inline
pH Level 8.5 - 9.5
Shelf Life 12 Months

Precision Engineered for Polyolefins

Formulating water-based inks for non-porous surfaces like Polypropylene (PP) and Polyethylene (PE) requires advanced polymer science. Our proprietary resin technology ensures immediate surface wetting and robust film formation upon drying, completely eliminating the need for aggressive solvent primers.

verified_user Food-Safe Formulation

Manufactured under strict ISO 9001 controls, utilizing raw materials compliant with major global regulations for indirect food contact. Contains zero heavy metals and exceptionally low VOCs.

layers Superior Rub Resistance

Once cross-linked, the ink film exhibits exceptional mechanical durability, resisting abrasion during high-speed cup nesting, stacking, and transportation processes.

palette Vibrant Color Density

High pigment loading delivers striking opacity and brilliant hues even on translucent or dark substrates, minimizing ink laydown requirements and improving cost-in-use.
