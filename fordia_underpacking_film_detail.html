Plate Underlay Film

FORDIA Plate Underpacking Film

Engineered for dimensional stability in long-run commercial printing. Our self-adhesive polyester film provides precise gauge control and superior chemical resistance.

Engineered for High-Precision Printing

Advanced polymer construction delivers unmatched stability and protection for modern offset presses.

layers

Self-Adhesive Design

Strong initial tack ensures secure placement, while specialized adhesive allows for clean removal without leaving residue on cylinders.

shield

Anticorrosion Protection

Formulated with active anti-corrosion inhibitors to actively protect valuable press roller surfaces from oxidation and wear.

science

Chemical Resistance

Exceptional resistance to aggressive printing solvents, automated blanket washes, and typical pressroom cleaning agents.

timer

Long Service Life

High-density polyester core maintains precise caliper under sustained pressure, reducing the need for frequent replacement.
