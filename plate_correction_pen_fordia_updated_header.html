FORDIA Plate Correction Pen

A precision-engineered tool designed for the immediate and accurate deletion of unwanted image areas on printing plates, ensuring zero residue and pristine print quality.

Technical Advantages

Engineered for absolute control in high-stakes manufacturing environments where minor imperfections lead to significant material waste.

edit_square

Sharp Correction

Delivers micro-precise chemical application allowing operators to target specific dots or lines without affecting adjacent image areas.

cleaning_services

Zero Residue

Formulated to evaporate cleanly, leaving the corrected area perfectly desensitized and ready for immediate press operation without ghosting.

speed

Rapid Action

Active chemical agents break down unwanted emulsion in seconds, significantly reducing press downtime during plate corrections.

Technical Specifications

Tip Size Fine (0.5mm), Medium (1.0mm), Broad (2.0mm)
Compatibility Analog and most positive/negative thermal CTP plates
Volume 15ml per unit
Shelf Life 12 months (stored in cool, dry conditions)
Packaging Box of 10 units
