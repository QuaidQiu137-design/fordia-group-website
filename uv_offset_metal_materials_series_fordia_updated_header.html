Product Series: Metal Packaging Solutions

UV Offset Metal Materials Series

Advanced UV-curable inks engineered for exceptional adhesion to non-absorbent metallic substrates. Delivering instant cure times and superior scratch resistance for premium metal packaging and decorative tins.

Superior Adhesion for Challenging Substrates

The UV Offset Metal Materials Series represents a breakthrough in chemical formulation for non-absorbent surfaces. Specifically engineered for tinplate, aluminum, and treated stainless steel, these inks bypass the limitations of traditional oxidative drying.

Utilizing high-reactivity photoinitiators, the series forms an immediate, highly cross-linked polymer network upon UV exposure. This mechanism ensures zero set-off and eliminates the need for spray powders or intermediate drying zones on the press line, drastically improving throughput for metal decorators.
