LED UV Curing Offset Ink for Tinplate Label

Advanced LED-curable ink specifically engineered for the high-volume metal packaging industry. Delivers exceptional adhesion and vibrant color reproduction while drastically reducing energy consumption through low-heat, high-speed curing technology.

Engineering Advantages

energy_savings_leaf

Eco-Friendly Efficiency

Optimized for LED UV systems, requiring significantly less power than traditional mercury lamps. Zero VOC emissions during the curing process.

layers

Superior Metal Adhesion

Formulated with advanced cross-linking polymers to ensure unmatched bond strength on treated tinplate and aluminum substrates.

speed

High-Speed Curing

Instantly reacts to LED wavelengths, allowing for maximum line speeds without compromising print quality or structural integrity.

palette

High Pigment Load

Delivers intense, vibrant colors with lower ink film thickness, improving cost-efficiency per unit printed.
