UV Flexographic Ink for Paper and Label

Precision-engineered curable formulations delivering uncompromising speed, vibrant dot reproduction, and exceptional substrate adherence for professional high-volume label converting.

High-Performance Formulation

Our advanced UV-curable flexographic ink is strictly optimized for high-speed application on paper substrates and self-adhesive label stock. Engineered to deliver excellent dot reproduction and an unparalleled high-gloss finish, this low-odor formulation ensures operational compliance while maximizing press uptime and final print fidelity.
