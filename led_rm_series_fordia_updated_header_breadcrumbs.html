Printing Inks

LED-RM Series

High-sensitivity LED-curable series specifically engineered for lottery tickets and thermal receipts. Optimized for high-speed instant drying without compromising the thermal-sensitive layer.

Technical Advantages

bolt

Energy Efficient Curing

Advanced LED-curable formulation significantly reduces energy consumption compared to traditional UV curing methods, lowering operational costs.

contrast

High Contrast

Delivers exceptionally sharp, high-contrast imagery vital for barcodes and legibility on lottery tickets and retail receipts.

speed

High-Speed Processing

Formulated for instant drying to support modern, high-throughput printing lines without smearing or offsetting.

science

Thermal Layer Safe

Specifically designed to cure without prematurely activating or degrading the sensitive coating on thermal paper substrates.
