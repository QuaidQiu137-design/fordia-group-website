FORDIA Blanket Restorer

Advanced chemical formulation designed to rejuvenate and restore the surface of printing blankets, extending operational lifespan and ensuring consistent print precision.

View Technical Data arrow_downward

Technical Advantages

Engineered for high-volume offset printing environments, FORDIA Blanket Restorer penetrates deep into the rubber matrix to reverse oxidation and compression set.

restore

Restores Elasticity & Resilience

Actively breaks down hardened ink residue and paper dust while replenishing essential plasticizers within the blanket rubber. This process recovers original surface tension and smash resistance, critical for sharp dot transfer.
