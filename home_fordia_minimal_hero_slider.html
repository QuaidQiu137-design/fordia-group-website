About FORDIA

Pioneering Industrial Excellence Since 1995.

FORDIA has been engaged in the offset printing industry since 1995 and has 30 years of professional industry experience. We mainly produce offset printing inks and various offset printing consumables and chemicals. We design highly efficient printing products specifically for the graphic arts industry. Our solutions boost your productivity, save you time and money, and fuel innovation to keep you competitive. We empower your business to thrive. We serve more than 1,000 customers, including well-known domestic and foreign companies such as ShenZhen YUTO Packaging, RR Donnelley, Stora Enso, etc. Choose FORDIA as your strongest partner for business success.
