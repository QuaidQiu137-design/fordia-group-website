Technical Specifications & Applications

Our pure cellulose sponges are manufactured to exacting tolerances, ensuring consistent performance across critical industrial applications.

Pure Cellulose Composition

Manufactured from 100% pure, unadulterated cellulose material, ensuring zero synthetic contamination and maximum structural integrity under stress.

High Fluid Retention

Engineered micro-porous structure absorbs and holds up to 20 times its dry weight in fluids, providing consistent distribution over extended periods.

Precision Plate Damping

The industry standard for lithographic and offset printing plate damping. The uniform cell structure guarantees even moisture application, preventing dry spots and ensuring flawless print quality run after run.
