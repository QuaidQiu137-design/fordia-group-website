UV High Gloss Over Printing Varnish for Film

A specialized high-gloss formulation engineered for exceptional adhesion on film substrates, delivering a premium, ultra-clear protective finish for demanding flexible packaging applications.

Product Overview

Our UV High Gloss Over Printing Varnish is specifically formulated to meet the rigorous demands of the flexible packaging industry. Utilizing advanced polymer technology, this varnish ensures superior wet-out and exceptional adhesion across a wide variety of film substrates, including PE, PP, and PET. It cures rapidly under standard UV systems to form a highly durable, flexible layer that resists cracking while imparting a brilliant, non-yellowing gloss finish. Ideal for enhancing the visual appeal and protective qualities of premium food, beverage, and consumer goods packaging.

Technical Advantages
