UV Offset Fluorescent Series

Engineered for high-impact visibility and unparalleled color consistency. Formulated with ultra-bright pigments offering superior stability under rigorous commercial and security printing conditions.

visibility

High Visibility

Formulated to reflect maximum UV light, resulting in intense, eye-catching color saturation that commands attention in any lighting condition.

tune

Sharp Dot Reproduction

Optimized rheology ensures precise ink transfer, maintaining edge acuity and preventing dot gain even at high press speeds.

  • Curing Speed Up to 15,000 sph
  • Viscosity (25°C) 35-45 Poise

  • Substrate Compatibility Paper, Board, Foil
