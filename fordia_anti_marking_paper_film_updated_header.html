Product Detail

FORDIA Anti-marking paper/film

Advanced engineered materials designed to eliminate ink scuffing and transfer marking in high-speed printing environments.

Uncompromising Surface Protection

In precision transfer processes, ink scuffing and offsetting are critical failure points. Our Anti-marking Paper and Film utilize engineered micro-textures and low surface energy coatings to create an ink-repellent barrier. This ensures pristine printed surfaces, reducing waste and machine downtime.
