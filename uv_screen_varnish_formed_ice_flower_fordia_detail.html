UV Screen Varnish Formed Ice Flower

A specialized decorative varnish engineered for high-end cosmetic and electronics packaging. Delivers a highly tactile, crystalline finish with exceptional surface durability and instant UV curing.

Technical Advantages

Engineered for precision and aesthetic impact in high-volume manufacturing environments.

touch_app

Distinctive Tactile Finish

Creates a highly unique 'ice flower' crystalline texture that elevates product packaging aesthetics and user engagement through touch.

bolt

Instant UV Curing

Formulated for rapid polymerization under standard industrial UV lamps, ensuring high throughput and immediate handling post-application.

verified_user

Exceptional Durability

Provides a robust, scratch-resistant surface layer that protects underlying print and substrate integrity throughout the product lifecycle.

Technical Specifications

Parameter Value / Standard
Appearance Milky white to semi-transparent viscous liquid
Viscosity (cps @ 25°C) 3500 - 5000
Curing Energy (mJ/cm²) > 800
Recommended Coating Weight 15 - 25 g/m²
Mesh Size Recommendation 120 - 200 mesh/inch
Substrate Compatibility Coated paper, PVC, PET, selected metals
