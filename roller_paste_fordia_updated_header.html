FORDIA Roller Paste

Advanced maintenance paste engineered to deeply condition and preserve ink rollers, maintaining optimal suppleness and peak receptivity for precision printing applications.

Technical Advantages

Formulated with advanced conditioning agents to combat the harsh environment of modern industrial printing.

layers_clear

Prevents Glaze Formation

Actively dissolves dried ink residues and paper dust that contribute to roller glazing. Regular application prevents the hardening of the roller surface, ensuring consistent ink transfer and extending the operational life of the rubber compound.

water_drop

Restores Suppleness

Deep-penetrating conditioners rejuvenate elastomers, preventing cracking and premature aging.

swap_horiz

Improves Ink Transfer

Maintains the ideal surface tension and texture, allowing for flawless ink reception and release.

speed

Reduces Downtime

By keeping rollers in optimal condition, FORDIA paste significantly reduces the frequency of full roller replacements and unscheduled maintenance stops.
