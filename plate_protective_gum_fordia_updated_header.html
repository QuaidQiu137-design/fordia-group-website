Protective Coatings Division

FORDIA Plate Protective Gum

Advanced protective coating engineered for precision. Formulated to deliver uncompromising short and long-term plate storage security, ensuring optimal performance upon reactivation.

Technical Advantages

FORDIA Plate Protective Gum sets the industry standard for plate preservation, combining robust protection with operational efficiency.

layers

Excellent Film Forming

Creates a uniform, continuous barrier that isolates the plate surface from oxidation, moisture, and environmental contaminants during storage.

water_drop

Easy Wash Off

Formulated for rapid solubility upon reactivation. The coating rinses away cleanly with standard preparation procedures, leaving no residue.

schedule

Extended Stability

Maintains integrity over prolonged periods without cracking or flaking. Suitable for both immediate staging and deep archival storage.

Use Cases

Primary Applications

  • check_circle Preservation of thermal and violet photopolymer printing plates post-processing.
  • check_circle Protection against scratches and handling damage during inter-departmental transport.
  • check_circle Archival storage requiring high-barrier resistance to ambient humidity fluctuations.
