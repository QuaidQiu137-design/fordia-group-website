UVLED Screen Ink for Aluminum

Specialized ink for metallic substrates, offering superior chemical resistance and adhesion for industrial nameplates and precision manufactured parts.

Product Overview

Engineered specifically for the demands of industrial manufacturing, our UVLED Screen Ink provides an uncompromising bond on bare, treated, or brushed aluminum surfaces. Formulated with advanced photopolymers, it cures instantly under UVLED lamps, accelerating production lines while ensuring a durable, chemically resistant finish.
