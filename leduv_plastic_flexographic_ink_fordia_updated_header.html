LEDUV Plastic Flexographic Ink

Advanced Formulation

The LEDUV Plastic Flexographic Ink is an advanced LED-curable series specifically designed for energy-efficient production on heat-sensitive plastic films. It offers immediate curing under specialized LED wavelengths, ensuring consistent rheology and exceptionally high color strength even at elevated press speeds.
