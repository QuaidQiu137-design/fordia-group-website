Product Family: Solvents

FORDIA Press Wash

Heavy-duty precision solvent engineered for rapid cleaning of critical press components. Formulated for high solvency and controlled evaporation.

Technical Advantages

speed

Rapid Evaporation

Optimized vapor pressure profile ensures quick drying times, reducing equipment downtime between production runs.

science

High Solvency

Aggressive chemical matrix breaks down stubborn UV and conventional inks without damaging underlying elastomer rollers.

water_drop

Residue-Free

Leaves behind a pristine surface with zero chemical film, ensuring optimal ink transfer on subsequent jobs.

shield

Component Safe

Engineered to be non-corrosive to standard press metals and safe for use on most modern photopolymer plates.
