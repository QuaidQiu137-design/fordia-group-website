Precision Engineered Brilliance

FORDIA Metallic Pantone Inks are formulated using cutting-edge resin technology and highly refined metallic pigments. This ensures an unparalleled metallic luster that resists oxidation and tarnishing over time. The precise rheology of the ink allows for clean printing on high-speed automated lines without stringing or misting.

Designed specifically for the automotive, electronics, and premium packaging sectors, these inks offer excellent adhesion to treated polyolefins, metals, and coated glass. They are fully compliant with RoHS and REACH directives, ensuring global usability.
