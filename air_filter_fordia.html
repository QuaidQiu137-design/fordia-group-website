Maintaining a Clean Printing Environment

In modern high-speed printing environments, airborne particulates and solvent fumes pose significant risks to both equipment longevity and print quality. Our Air Filter series is meticulously engineered to address these challenges at the source.

Utilizing multi-stage HEPA and activated carbon filtration, these units capture micro-particles and neutralize volatile organic compounds (VOCs). The result is a stabilized press room atmosphere that minimizes maintenance downtime and ensures consistent, flawless production runs.

  • check_circle Removes 99.97% of airborne particulates down to 0.3 microns.
  • check_circle Active VOC neutralization via dense carbon layering.
  • check_circle Modular design for seamless integration into existing HVAC lines.

High-Efficiency Filtration

Multi-tiered media captures nano-scale ink mist and paper dust, preventing accumulation on sensitive sensor optics and moving mechanical components.

Airflow Optimization

Aerodynamically designed internal baffling reduces static pressure drop, maintaining high CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute) throughput with lower energy consumption.

Smart Monitoring

Integrated differential pressure sensors automatically alert maintenance teams when filter media approaches capacity, preventing unexpected workflow interruptions.

Press Room Ventilation Solutions

Our units are specifically tailored for the demanding layouts of commercial offset and digital press rooms. By establishing targeted extraction zones above high-emission areas, we prevent the dispersion of contaminants throughout the facility.
